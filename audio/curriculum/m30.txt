Welcome to Section 4: Strategies for Resilience and Coping!

When discussing climate-related emotions, it is important to explore how people cope with and respond to these experiences. Responses to climate change can vary widely, ranging from feelings of hope, connection, and empowerment to overwhelm, avoidance, and distress. These responses are shaped not only by individual experiences and coping styles, but also by broader social, cultural, political, and environmental contexts.

This section of the course explores different ways people respond to climate emotions, including more adaptive and less adaptive coping strategies, the role of hope and resilience, and a range of therapeutic and supportive approaches that can help individuals and communities navigate climate-related distress. It also highlights the importance of collective support, social systems, and climate justice frameworks in addressing the emotional and mental health impacts of climate change and fostering meaningful resilience and action.
