Section 1: Overview of Climate Change and Its Impacts
Audio Overview — Transcript

This module provides an overview of what climate change is, and how it impacts individuals, society and the planet. The term climate change refers to the irreversible long-term changes in temperature, precipitation, and other atmospheric conditions on the planet. These changes can be natural, but since the 1800s have largely been driven by human activities such as burning fossil fuels. These changes have far-reaching impacts on the environment, ecosystems, humans, and planetary health. Some of the key impacts of climate change include rising sea levels, extreme heat and cold events, food and water insecurity, increased range and spread of vector-borne diseases, and more frequent and intense extreme weather events, such as class 5+ hurricanes.

The concept of planetary health helps to gain an understanding of climate change as a broader human and ecological health issue, and the planetary boundaries act as a global health check that can help to gauge whether the Earth is remaining in a safe operating space.

Indigenous perspectives highlight how climate change reflects disrupted relationships, and the need for restoring balance and reciprocity

In this module, you can review these impacts to understand the broad ways by which climate change affects us, our communities, and nations now and is anticipated to impact us in the future. We will also reflect on how we can impact climate change and prepare ourselves for the future.
