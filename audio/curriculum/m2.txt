Welcome to Section 1: Introduction to Climate Change and Mental Health!

When we consider the impacts of climate change, it is important to understand the far reaching effects, not only on the environment, but all living things. In the case of humans, climate change not only alters our physical surroundings but also profoundly affects our psychological well-being, influencing how we perceive and respond to the changing world around us.

In this section, we provide an overview of climate change and its widespread impacts on ecosystems, the environment, human societies, and health. We also review the importance of understanding the connections between climate change and mental health at the individual, community, and systemic levels.

It is also essential to understand how climate change connects to the health and well-being of Indigenous Peoples and some of the many ways Indigenous Peoples are leading stewardship, regeneration, and protection of lands, waters, plants, animals, and climate well-being.

Finally, we review major frameworks as well as cultural and Indigenous perspectives which relate to a more holistic approach to mental health and well-being.
